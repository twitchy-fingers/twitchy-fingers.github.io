About

I work on the part of security that decides who gets access to what, on which device, from where, and for how long. Most of my day goes to joiner, mover, and leaver automation, Conditional Access and MFA, SSO and SCIM integrations, and access reviews that satisfy PCI DSS and NIST SP 800-53.

I care about least privilege and about explaining access decisions clearly enough that the people they affect understand why. Outside of client work, I build enterprise-style identity labs to practice and document what I learn.
